Sources & method

Figures are calculated from the three supplied workbooks, using the canonical source table in each. Transaction Time defines activity time, with Entry Time used only when unavailable.

Workbook / tableAccepted records

44,236 accepted records · 28 exact duplicates removed · 4 invalid/report rows excluded. Zero-dollar records remain in activity counts.

Location grouping: source domains group related meters for this concept. They are not confirmed physical-lot boundaries. All map positions are illustrative and must be replaced with reviewed links to the existing location registry.

Interpretation: LocoMobi amounts have no supplied tax breakdown. HotSpot / QR totals are not combined here. Occupancy, stay length and turnover cannot be calculated from this archive.

The demo recalculates area, monthly and hourly aggregates for the selected period. It does not write to the production workspace.
