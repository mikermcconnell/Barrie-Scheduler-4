Review items

Methodology

Exact duplicate rows and workbook summary rows were excluded. Amounts are source reported and are not labelled tax-inclusive revenue.

Privacy boundary

This concept uses only aggregates. Plates, cards, receipts, usernames, permits, and transaction identifiers are not present.

Strategy interpretation

Payment counts can identify where and when study may be valuable. Occupancy, duration, capacity, enforcement, rates, and cost data are required for policy conclusions.
