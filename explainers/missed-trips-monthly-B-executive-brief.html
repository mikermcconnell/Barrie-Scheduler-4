Barrie Transit · Monthly report
September 2026: 46 missed trips
99.7% of scheduled trips ran · down from 146 in August

Good month. Barrie Transit missed 46 of 15,857 scheduled trips (0.29%). That is 100 fewer than August. On 11 of 30 days, every trip ran.

Three things to know

  1. Some buses lost a run of trips. 8 buses each lost 2 or more trips in a row. Together they account for 23 of the 46 misses (50%).
  2. Weekends were weaker. Saturday (0.46%) and Sunday (0.50%) rates were about double the weekday rate (0.24%). Sept 5 and Sept 12 each lost 5 trips.
  3. Early mornings need watching. 12 misses (26%) were before 7 am, mostly on Routes 7 and 12. These are often pull-out or sign-on problems.

How we count a missed trip. A scheduled GTFS trip with no matching AVL/APC trip record that day ("not performed"), or a trip that started more than 15 minutes late. September had no trips in the late group.
When both directions of a route leave at the same minute, the miss is charged to the direction that had no bus.
