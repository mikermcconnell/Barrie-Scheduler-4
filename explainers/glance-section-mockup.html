What changed and why

  1. One headline sentence replaces the opening bullet. It says what kind of day it was. The date is already in the email header.
  2. "Usual" comparison. Each number shows the median of the last 20 weekdays (Saturdays and Sundays compare with their own). The KPI cards show the raw numbers; this section says if they were good or bad.
  3. What stood out lists only routes well below their own usual on-time (5+ points, with enough data). Yesterday that was 400, 101 and 15A. The current version mentions none of these on-time drops.
  4. Ongoing moves repeat issues into one grey box with how long they have lasted. A flag that is new today gets a "new" tag. Readers can skip this box on most days.
  5. 12A is no longer the headline. It is often the lowest route, so "lowest route" repeated every day. Now it shows only if 12A drops below its own usual.
  6. Action Focus items are numbered, colour-coded by severity, give counts and times, and link to the route in the dashboard. No ✓ marks, which made items look done.
  7. Quiet days stay short. When nothing stands out, the section says so in one line. This example uses made-up numbers.
Data: Oct 7 report data and 68 days of route history. "Usual" = median of Sep 9 – Oct 6 weekdays (20 days). Late trip = 25%+ of timepoints late. All numbers in the Oct 7 views are real.
