1You set the rankingDrag a card up or down. Drag a suggestion into the list to turn it on at that rank. The rank is saved with the route.
2Higher ranks are protectedThe optimizer works from #1 down. It skips any move that would break a higher-ranked connection that is already met, and it tells you why.
3You stay in controlSee the result per connection and on the timeline before you apply. Apply is one edit you can undo.
4Active hoursEach card has an "Active" setting: all day or a span such as a peak. Events outside it are greyed out and are not counted or optimized. Uses the existing timeFilterStart / timeFilterEnd fields.
5Badges in Manual tweaksEach trip row shows its connections as met / tight / missed. Move a trip with − / + and the badges and the header score update at once.
6Real running timesTurn it on to flag connections that are met on paper but that a late bus would miss (80th-percentile lateness from performance data). The optimizer can plan for it.
7GO and LINX times"+ Add connection" picks the source first. GO reads trains and GO buses from the Metrolinx GTFS feed the app already downloads, for the right day and station, and flags when the feed updates. LINX takes pasted times or a CSV now, and its GTFS feed later if Simcoe County publishes one. Both become cards you can rank, limit to hours, and optimize like any route.
