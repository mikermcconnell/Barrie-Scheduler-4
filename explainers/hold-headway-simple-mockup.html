Try it

  1. Look at the Target HDWY column. The grey dashed values are the system's estimates. In the evening the trips run 56, 36, 56, 36, and the estimate is 60.
  2. Hover an estimate to see how it was made: the block's cycle ÷ buses in service, rounded to a standard headway (10, 15, 20, 30, 60).
  3. Type over any target, for example 20 on a midday trip. Your value turns green and bold. The × next to it puts the estimate back.
  4. Click Fix headways. Recoveries change so each trip leaves at its target. Run times do not change.
  5. Click Show changes to see old recovery values. Undo puts the table back.
  6. Pick "PM peak 87" below the table, then Fix. Red recovery cells cannot fit the 5-min minimum. Click one for the three choices.
