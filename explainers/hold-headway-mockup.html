How to read this mock-up

  1. Before shows a fixed recovery on every trip. When the band run time changes, departures drift off the 30 and the evening alternates 30/60, as in your screenshot.
  2. After sets each last recovery so each block comes back on its cycle. The HDWY column holds the target.
  3. Red rows cannot fit the minimum recovery. Click one to see the three choices. The tool never changes them without asking.
  4. Amber rows have a long layover. This is normal after a change to 60 minutes with 2 blocks (cycle 120).
  5. At the period change, the tool ends the extra block that would get back last. The note in the panel names it.
