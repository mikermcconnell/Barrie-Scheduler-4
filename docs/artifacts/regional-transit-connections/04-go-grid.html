Reference, interpretation & proposed implementation
View the City's GO Train & Barrie Transit Connection Chart (PDF)
  • This mockup reproduces the weekday chart's train IDs, station times, route directions and local times. Other day types are not populated here. It preserves the earlier three concepts separately.
  • Each cell shows the bus arrival for “To GO”, or bus departure for “From GO”. The scheduled gap is train departure minus bus arrival, or bus departure minus train arrival. Values after midnight retain the next-day offset.
  • Use the printed chart's 1–5, 6–15 and 16–30 minute bands here. These differ from the earlier mockup's walk-adjusted assumptions. A positive gap does not establish a feasible transfer until walking and boarding requirements are checked.
  • A blank source cell means “not listed in chart”, not proof that the current schedule has no service. Times outside the 1–30 minute bands are flagged rather than silently corrected. For example, the source lists 11:42 PM / 11:52 PM for two buses under train 6911's 11:37 AM arrival.
  • Transit On-Demand is shown separately as a source service window, never counted as a timed bus connection. A service window does not guarantee a vehicle or a train connection. Fare information is intentionally not inferred or validated here.
  • Production would replace PDF transcription with current master trips and date-valid static GO GTFS. Confirm exact stop/platform pairs, route direction mappings, calendars, exceptions and overnight service; do not equate source labels such as 7A, 12A or 15A with current master routes automatically.
  • Keep all GO trip columns even when no connection is found. Distinguish no qualifying service from missing/expired data. Feed dates and master version IDs must be visible. Connection thresholds and walking allowances require planner approval.
